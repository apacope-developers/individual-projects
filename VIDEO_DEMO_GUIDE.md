# LifeLine Demo Video Production Guide

## Video Overview
A 3-minute professional demo video showcasing how LifeLine saves lives in emergency situations.

## Video Structure & Script

### Opening (0:00 - 0:20)
- **Visual**: Fast-paced montage of emergency scenarios (choking, heart attack, bleeding)
- **Voiceover**: "In an emergency, every second counts. But most people don't know what to do."
- **Text overlay**: "What if help was just a tap away?"
- **LifeLine logo animation appears**

### Problem Introduction (0:20 - 0:45)
- **Visual**: Person looking panicked, phone in hand
- **Voiceover**: "Medical emergencies happen without warning. 70% of people feel unprepared to help."
- **Screen recording**: Person searching "what to do for choking" - getting confusing results
- **Voiceover**: "Conflicting information online wastes precious time."

### LifeLine Solution (0:45 - 1:15)
- **Visual**: Clean interface of LifeLine app opening
- **Voiceover**: "Introducing LifeLine - your emergency first aid system."
- **Screen recordings**:
  - Quick login/register screen
  - Clean dashboard with emergency options
- **Voiceover**: "Instant access to professional medical guidance when you need it most."

### Feature 1: AI Symptom Checker (1:15 - 1:35)
- **Visual**: Person tapping "Symptom Checker"
- **Screen recording**: 
  - AI chat interface asking questions
  - Quick assessment showing "Critical - Call 911"
  - Immediate first aid steps appearing
- **Voiceover**: "AI-powered assessment prioritizes your situation and provides immediate guidance."

### Feature 2: Interactive Body Map (1:35 - 1:55)
- **Visual**: Person tapping different body parts
- **Screen recording**:
  - Tapping chest → Heart attack procedures
  - Tapping throat → Choking guidance
  - Tapping arm → Bleeding control
- **Voiceover**: "Click any body part for targeted emergency procedures."

### Feature 3: Step-by-Step First Aid (1:55 - 2:20)
- **Visual**: Clear, animated first aid instructions
- **Screen recordings**:
  - CPR instructions with visual animations
  - Heimlich maneuver demonstration
  - Burn treatment steps
- **Voiceover**: "Clear, visual instructions guide you through life-saving procedures."

### Feature 4: CPR Metronome (2:20 - 2:35)
- **Visual**: CPR metronome in action
- **Screen recording**:
  - 100-120 BPM audio playing
  - Visual compression guide
  - Timer showing elapsed time
- **Voiceover**: "Real-time CPR guidance ensures proper timing and technique."

### Feature 5: Emergency Contacts (2:35 - 2:50)
- **Visual**: One-tap emergency calling
- **Screen recording**:
  - Quick emergency services dialing
  - Hospital locator
  - Personal emergency contacts
- **Voiceover**: "One-tap access to emergency services when every second matters."

### Call to Action (2:50 - 3:00)
- **Visual**: Happy person feeling confident, LifeLine app on screen
- **Voiceover**: "Be prepared for any emergency. Download LifeLine today."
- **Text overlay**: "Save a life. Be ready."
- **App store badges and website URL

## Production Requirements

### Visual Style
- **Color scheme**: Match Lifeline branding (red, teal, dark theme)
- **Typography**: Clean, medical-grade fonts
- **Animations**: Smooth, professional transitions
- **Screen recordings**: High-quality, 1080p or higher

### Audio Requirements
- **Voiceover**: Professional, calm, authoritative voice
- **Background music**: Urgent but not panic-inducing
- **Sound effects**: Subtle UI sounds for interactions
- **Music licensing**: Royalty-free or licensed

### Technical Specs
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD) minimum
- **Frame rate**: 30fps or 60fps
- **Format**: MP4, H.264
- **Duration**: Exactly 3 minutes
- **File size**: Under 100MB for web

## Recording Guidelines

### Screen Recordings
1. Use a clean device (no notifications)
2. Record in a quiet environment
3. Use smooth mouse movements
4. Highlight important elements with subtle animations
5. Show realistic user interactions

### Live Action Shots
1. Use diverse actors (age, gender, ethnicity)
2. Professional lighting
3. Multiple camera angles
4. Focus on emotions (panic → confidence)
5. Medical accuracy in demonstrations

### Animation Requirements
1. 2D motion graphics for UI elements
2. Medical procedure animations (CPR, Heimlich)
3. Data visualization (statistics, timing)
4. Logo animations and transitions

## Content Guidelines

### Medical Accuracy
- All procedures must be medically accurate
- Follow Red Cross/WHO guidelines
- Include disclaimers for professional medical help
- Emphasize calling emergency services

### Emotional Tone
- Start with urgency/panic
- Transition to confidence/calm
- End with empowerment
- Avoid graphic or disturbing content

### Legal Compliance
- Include "This is not medical advice" disclaimer
- Show emergency services prominently
- Follow platform guidelines (YouTube, etc.)
- Respect privacy and consent
